Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the $25K audit? +

A full 3-week engagement covering all 8 PRS dimensions: accuracy/grounding, cost efficiency, security, reliability, observability, human-in-the-loop design, scalability, and compliance readiness. You get an executive summary, a detailed technical report, a prioritized remediation roadmap, and 30 days of post-audit support.

Can I start with the books and upgrade to consulting later? +

Absolutely. The books teach the same PRS framework we use in audits. Many clients read the series first, implement what they can internally, then engage us for the gaps that need expert assessment. Book buyers who upgrade to an audit get a $249 credit toward the engagement.

What does the enterprise book license include? +

Unlimited distribution of all 7 volumes within your organization for one year. Includes quarterly content updates as the AI landscape evolves, and priority email support for framework implementation questions. Perfect for AI Centers of Excellence and L&D training programs.

How quickly can we start the audit? +

Typically within 1-2 weeks of signing. We maintain limited engagement slots to ensure quality. Book a free 30-minute diagnostic first to scope the engagement and confirm fit.

Do you work with startups or only enterprise? +

We work with any organization running AI agents in production or preparing for production deployment. The books are designed for individual engineers and small teams. The audit and managed services are typically best suited for companies with 50+ employees or significant AI agent deployments.

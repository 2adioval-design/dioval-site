The Problem

Built for 2026. Not 2015.

Legacy certifications were designed before AI-powered attacks, multi-agent systems, post-quantum cryptography, and the EU AI Act existed. The CCO was built for the threat landscape you actually face.

AI-Powered Threats

Adversaries use LLMs for polymorphic malware, deepfake phishing, and automated vulnerability discovery. Your cert should cover this.

Cloud-Native Default

Multi-cloud, serverless, containers, ephemeral infrastructure. Legacy certs still diagram on-premise networks.

Multi-Agent Attack Surface

Enterprises deploy autonomous AI agents. These create entirely new attack vectors. No existing certification addresses this.

Post-Quantum Cryptography

NIST finalized PQC standards. The migration timeline is active. Legacy certs still focus on RSA and AES fundamentals.

Regulatory Explosion

EU AI Act, SR 11-7, DORA, NIS2. Compliance surface area tripled in two years. Legacy certs predate all of it.

Operational, Not Academic

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CCO certification? +

The Certified Cybersecurity Operator (CCO) is a three-level credential that tests operational capability through scenario-based exams and live practical labs. It was created by Dioval Group to address the gap between what traditional certifications test and what production security actually demands.

How is the CCO different from CISSP, OSCP, or Security+? +

Legacy certifications primarily test vocabulary recognition, framework memorization, and theoretical process recall through multiple-choice exams. The CCO tests whether you can actually operate: architect Zero Trust environments, bypass active EDR in live labs, threat-hunt APTs under time pressure, and deliver professional-grade reports. See the comparison table above for a detailed breakdown.

Do I need to take the levels in order? +

CCO-F is a prerequisite for both CCO-A and CCO-D. However, CCO-A and CCO-D are independent tracks. You can pursue either or both based on whether your career focus is offensive (red team) or defensive (blue team).

Is there a study guide? +

Each CCO level aligns with a volume of The Agentic AI Professional Series. CCO-F maps to Book 1: The Foundation. CCO-A maps to Book 2: The Arsenal. CCO-D maps to Book 3: The Bastion. The books serve as the official study guide, but the exam tests application, not memorization.

What does the beta cohort include? +

50 free CCO-F exam vouchers for experienced security professionals. Beta members also get early access to CCO-A and CCO-D when they launch. The only requirements are providing detailed feedback on the exam experience and sharing the credential publicly if earned.

What happens if I fail? +

Each voucher includes one exam attempt. Retake vouchers are available at a discounted rate. For beta cohort members, we will provide detailed feedback on areas for improvement.

When does the CCO launch? +

The CCO-F beta launches with the first cohort of 50 applicants. CCO-A and CCO-D are in development and will be announced to all CCO-F holders first. Contact us or follow on X for updates.

Can my employer pay for the certification? +

Yes. We offer enterprise invoicing and team pricing for organizations that want to certify multiple employees. Contact us for details.
